The Experience

Where Lagos
comes alive

Aurelia was conceived as an antidote to spectacle — a space where the architecture does the seducing. Bathed in low amber light, with hand-poured concrete walls and a sound system tuned to a frequency you feel before you hear, every evening here is a study in deliberate pleasure.

We do not advertise. We do not need to. Our guests return because what happens inside Aurelia cannot be replicated outside it.

Our Story

01 — The Vision

Built for those
who know

Aurelia began as a conversation between two architects and a chef, in a quiet corner of an Ikoyi bar that no longer exists. The question they kept returning to was this: why does Lagos, one of the great nocturnal cities of the world, not yet have a room worthy of it?

That question became a four-year obsession, a warehouse on Victoria Island, and eventually the room you enter tonight. Aurelia opened in 2019 without a launch party, without a press release. It filled by reputation alone within three weeks.

02 — The Space

Designed to
disorient gently

The interior was designed by Studio Ọba, working with poured concrete, raw brass, and 400-year-old Iroko beams salvaged from a demolished port warehouse. The main floor holds 220 guests at full occupancy; it rarely feels that full.

The sound system — a custom-built Funktion-One rig tuned to the room's specific acoustic geometry — is arguably the finest in sub-Saharan Africa. It is loud enough to feel, quiet enough to permit conversation.

Common Questions

FAQ

What is the entry policy?+

Aurelia operates a strict members-preferred door policy. Walk-in access is possible Wednesday through Thursday but not guaranteed. Friday through Saturday are table reservation nights only. All guests are subject to management approval at the door, regardless of reservation status.

Is there an age restriction?+

Aurelia is strictly 21 years and older. Valid government-issued photo identification will be requested at the door for all guests who appear under 30. There are no exceptions to this policy.

What is the dress code?+

Smart elegant at all times. Tailored suits, blazers, evening gowns, and cocktail dresses are the standard. Trainers, shorts, ripped denim, and sportswear will not be admitted. Traditional Nigerian attire — Agbada, Senator, and contemporary interpretations — is warmly welcomed. Management reserves the right to refuse entry at their discretion.

Is there parking available?+

Aurelia has valet parking for VIP table guests. The valet service operates from 9:30 PM and is complimentary with Booth and Penthouse reservations. Lounge Table guests receive a discounted rate. A public car park is available 200 metres from the venue on Adeola Odeku Street for walk-in guests.

How do I secure a table reservation?+

Complete the reservation form on our VIP page or contact us directly via WhatsApp. Our team will respond within 12 hours to confirm availability and collect a 30% deposit to secure your table. All deposits are non-refundable within 48 hours of the event date.

Can I host a private event at Aurelia?+

Aurelia is available for private hire on Mondays and Tuesdays, and for partial buyouts on certain weekday evenings. We have hosted product launches, intimate concerts, and private dinners for up to 80 guests. Contact our events team directly at reservations@aurelialagos.com for capacity and pricing.
